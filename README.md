## 🌟 Overview

MMO Challenge is a web-based simulation platform that lets users experience four major online income paths in a risk-free environment. Unlike typical tutorials, this platform puts you under **real pressure** with limited resources, time constraints, and fail conditions.

**This is NOT a get-rich-quick guide. It's a reality check.**

## 🎮 Features

### 💼 Four MMO Paths

| Path | Challenge | Fail Condition |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| **Freelance** | 40 hours to complete jobs | Run out of time or reputation |
| **Affiliate Marketing** | 14 days to reach 100 visitors | Miss deadline |
| **Dropshipping** | 10 ad campaigns only | Refund rate exceeds 30% |
| **Trading/Crypto** | 15 trades max | 3 risk rule violations |

### ⚡ Resource Management

- **Energy** - Depletes with each action
- **Stress** - Increases with risky decisions
- **Time** - 7 days to prove yourself

### 📊 Scoring System

- **Strategy Score** - Measures smart decision-making
- **Risk Score** - Tracks risk management discipline
- **Overall Grade** - A+ to F based on performance

### 🌐 Bilingual Support

Switch between **English** and **Vietnamese** with one click.

## 📖 How to Play

1. **Start Challenge** - Begin with $1,000 virtual balance
2. **Choose a Path** - Select one of four MMO challenges
3. **Make Decisions** - Each action has consequences
4. **Manage Resources** - Don't burn out!
5. **Survive 7 Days** - Complete the challenge
6. **Get Your Grade** - See your final score

## 🏆 Achievements

Unlock badges based on your performance:

| Badge | Name | Requirement |
|-------|------|-------------|
